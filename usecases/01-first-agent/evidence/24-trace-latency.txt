returned is: <= 5 s, <= 5 s, <= 107 s. Not the 2-3 minutes the CLI hint warns
about. All three bounds are upper bounds; only the <= 5 s figures are tight,
because those polls started 5 s after the invoke returned and were immediately
positive.

Caveat: the very first invokes after a fresh deploy are slower to show up than
later ones, and "Transaction search enabled. It takes ~10 minutes for
transaction search to be fully active" is printed by every deploy. The numbers
above are from a stack that had been up for about 20 minutes, not from the
first minute after deploy.
